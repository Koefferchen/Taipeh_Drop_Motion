
\section{Experimental Setup}
    \label{sec:Experimental_Setup}

    To investigate the influence of the substrate geometry on static and dynamic observables, we place water droplets on PMMA cylinders with diameters $D \in \{ \num{2.19(2)},\, \num{3.99(2)},\, \num{5.15(4)},\, \num{10.2(2)} \} \, \text{mm}$, using a high-precision mechanical pipette by \textit{Sartorius} for $V \in [2.0, \, 20.0] \, \mu \text{L}$. 
    \begin{figure}[H]
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../Figs/photo_samples.PNG}
        \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../Figs/photo_pipette.PNG}
        \caption{Photo of PMMA samples and fixation plate (top). The cylinders' back sides are painted black to enhance contrast for measurements of $W$. Below: the \SI{2}{\micro\liter} to \SI{20}{\micro\liter} pipette used with \textit{KG1213-L} Low Binding Pipette Tips.}
        \label{fig:photo_samples}
    \end{figure}
    The cylinder is fixed on a movable platform using rubber bands and the observation is supported by a white backlight. All experimemts use twice-boiled tap water for the liquid droplets. 
    \begin{figure}[H]
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=0.41\linewidth]{../Figs/photo_measure_front.PNG}
        \includegraphics[width=0.56\linewidth]{../Figs/photo_measure_side.PNG}
        \caption{Photo from the camera perspective to measure $W(x)$ (left) and $L$ (right) on the \SI{10}{mm} cylinder fixed by rubber bands.}
        \label{fig:photo_measure}
    \end{figure}
    Finally, imaging was performed using the \textit{MEMRECAM GX-1} camera (C Mount Depth \SI{9}{mm}) with a narrow lens and fixed focus for upto 4x magnification. The high-speed camera is operated by the software \textit{MLink} and set to 50~FPS and a shutter speed of 1/250~s. The image format is set to $484$x$480$. To analyze the snapshots, the distances $L$ and $x$ are measured in pixels using the software \textit{ImageJ}. After each measurement, a pipe of known width is placed in the focus of the lens to gauge the pixel size for a given magnification. 
    \begin{figure}[H]
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=0.9\linewidth]{../Figs/photo_camera_side.jpg}
        \includegraphics[width=0.9\linewidth]{../Figs/photo_lens.PNG}
        \caption{Photo of the high-speed camera (top) and the 4x magnification lens (bottom).}
        \label{fig:photo_camera}
    \end{figure}
    Between measurements, the sample cylinders are dried and cleaned of dust using \textit{Kimwipes$^{\text{TM}}$} for professional use. While the humidity in the laboratory cannot be controlled accurately, the room temperature during the experiment lies between \SI{25}{\celsius} and \SI{29}{\celsius}. The backlight is not expected to contribute meaningfully to evaporation as it is not focused.
